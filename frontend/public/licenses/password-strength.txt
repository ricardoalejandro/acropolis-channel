Dictionary data attribution

The English and Spanish common-word dictionaries are generated from
the OpenSubtitles 2024 dataset provided through OPUS:
https://opus.nlpl.eu/datasets/OpenSubtitles
https://opus.nlpl.eu/

OpenSubtitles 2024 is licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution
License (ODC-BY): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/
The distributed word lists derive from that dataset. They are used
locally to estimate password strength; the estimator's feedback is
translated into Spanish by the zxcvbn-ts Spanish language package.
